Known limits (not bugs)

  • Agamya does not send email. Invitations open your own Gmail or copy a link.
  • Work Map steps need AI. If AI is unavailable, sessions are typed and the map page says so.
  • Narration in languages other than English, Hindi, Marathi and Kannada is labelled "not yet reviewed by a native speaker".
  • A practice result is practice, not a certification.
  • Screen sharing needs a computer browser. On phones, use the camera.
