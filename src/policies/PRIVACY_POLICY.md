# Privacy Policy

- **What we collect:** your account details; what you explicitly capture (screen or camera frames when they change, your voice, documents); your answers; practice results; usage and audit records.
- **Why:** to capture, confirm and teach work knowledge, to keep the service secure, and to show whether teaching works.
- **Where:** on Google Cloud (Cloud SQL, Cloud Storage and Gemini on Vertex AI) in the region where Agamya runs.
- **Personal details in speech** (emails, phone numbers, card numbers, national identity numbers) are masked before transcripts are stored.
- **How long:** recordings are kept for review for 30 days by default; transcripts longer; confirmed knowledge and audit records until they are removed. Space owners can change retention.
- **Your controls:** go off the record, stop, delete any captured moment, terminate a session, reject anything the AI drafted, and choose who your knowledge is shared with.
- **AI processing:** content is processed by Google's Gemini models on Vertex AI to provide the service. It is not used by Agamya to train models.
