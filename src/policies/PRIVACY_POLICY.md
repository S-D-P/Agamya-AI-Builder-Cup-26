# Privacy Policy (template)

- **What is collected:** account details; content you explicitly capture (screen frames, camera frames, audio, documents); your answers; learner responses; usage and audit logs.
- **Why:** to capture, validate and teach work knowledge; to secure the service; to measure training effectiveness.
- **Where:** Google Cloud (Cloud SQL, Cloud Storage, Vertex AI) in the region configured by your organization.
- **Retention:** raw media is short-lived by default; transcripts medium; validated knowledge and audit records longer. Your organization's administrators configure retention.
- **Your controls:** pause capture, exclude a modality, delete a captured moment, review and reject AI-extracted knowledge.
- **AI processing:** content is sent to Google's Gemini models on Vertex AI for processing. See the AI Usage Policy.
